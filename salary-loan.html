In-Depth Guide

Everything You Need to Know About Salary Based Loans

Understand eligibility, interest rates, documentation, and key benefits before applying for a salary loan with LoanLoop.

What is a Salary Based Loan?

A Salary Based Loan is an unsecured personal loan extended to salaried employees based on their monthly salary credit, employer profile, and credit standing. Unlike mortgage or vehicle loans, a salary loan requires no guarantor or asset collateral. Lenders evaluate your repayment capacity directly from your regular monthly paycheck.

Whether you require urgent funds for medical emergencies, home renovation, wedding expenses, education fees, or credit card debt consolidation, a salary loan gives you quick liquidity without risking personal assets.

Eligibility Criteria for Salaried Employees

Lenders maintain simple eligibility benchmarks for salary loans:

  • Min Monthly Income: ₹15,000/month (Higher income qualifies for larger loan amounts).
  • Age Limit: 21 to 60 years at the time of loan maturity.
  • Work Experience: Minimum 6 months in current organization or overall 1 year experience.
  • Employment Status: Salaried employee in Govt, PSU, Private Ltd, MNC, LLP, or Public Ltd firm.
  • Credit Score: CIBIL score of 650+ (750+ unlocks super prime interest rates).

Required Documents Checklist

Keep these documents ready for seamless 24-hour loan approval:

  • Identity Proof: PAN Card (Mandatory) + Aadhaar Card / Passport / Voter ID.
  • Address Proof: Aadhaar Card, Recent Utility Bill, or Rental Agreement.
  • Income Proof: Latest 3 months Salary Slips with official company seal or digital pay slip.
  • Bank Statement: Latest 6 months bank statement showing salary credits.
  • Employment Proof: Office ID Card / HR Official Email verification.
