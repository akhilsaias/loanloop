What is a Home Loan Balance Transfer?

A Home Loan Balance Transfer (or Home Loan Takeover) is a financial provision that allows homeowners to transfer their outstanding home loan principal balance from their current bank or NBFC to a new financial institution. Borrowers typically opt for balance transfers to secure significantly lower interest rates, obtain better customer service, or access high-value top-up loans.

With real estate markets expanding across major cities like Hyderabad, Visakhapatnam, Vijayawada, and Warangal, switching to modern floating or fixed interest rates through LoanLoop can yield massive long-term savings.

Eligibility Criteria for Balance Transfer

To qualify for a smooth home loan balance transfer with partner banks, applicants should meet:

  • Repayment Track Record: Minimum 12 uninterrupted EMIs paid on the current home loan.
  • Credit Score: CIBIL score of 720 or higher is preferred for maximum interest reduction.
  • Employment Status: Salaried professionals with steady income or self-employed individuals with active business history.
  • Property Title: Clear and marketable legal title with complete property documentation.

Key Documents Required

Keep these essential documents ready for an accelerated balance transfer approval:

  • Identity & Address Proof: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID.
  • Income Proof: Latest 3 months payslips, 6 months bank statements, and Form 16 / ITR.
  • Existing Loan Details: Outstanding principal balance statement and 12-month EMI track record from the current bank.
  • LOD (List of Documents): Copy of property documents held by the existing bank.
