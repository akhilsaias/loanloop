Pension Loan Guide

Complete Guide to Pension Loans in AP & Telangana

Everything senior citizens and retirees need to know about eligibility, documentation, and key benefits.

What is a Pension Loan?

A Pension Loan is a specialized personal loan designed exclusively for senior citizens and retired individuals who draw a monthly pension. Since regular personal loans often have age restrictions, pension loans leverage the guaranteed monthly pension credited to the retiree's bank account to grant collateral-free credit.

LoanLoop acts as an authorized channel partner connecting senior citizens across Andhra Pradesh and Telangana with leading PSU and private banks to ensure a smooth, quick, and comfortable application experience.

Key Features & Benefits

  • High Loan Amount: Get up to 18 to 20 times your monthly net pension (up to ₹14 Lakhs).
  • Low Interest Rates: Enjoy lower interest rates compared to regular unsecured personal loans.
  • No Collateral Required: Zero security or mortgage required against your property.
  • Flexible Tenure: Repay conveniently with tenures ranging from 12 to 84 months (up to 76 years of age).
  • Hassle-Free Processing: Simple documentation with auto-debit facility from pension bank account.

Eligibility Criteria for Pensioners

To qualify for a pension loan through LoanLoop's partner banks, applicants must meet the following general standards:

  • Pensioner Type: Central Government, State Government, Defence, Railway, or Family Pensioner.
  • Age Limit: Must be below 76 years at the time of loan tenure completion.
  • Pension Account: Monthly pension must be credited through a designated partner bank branch in AP or Telangana.
  • PPO Availability: Must possess a valid Pension Payment Order (PPO) number.

Required Documents Checklist

Keep these standard documents handy for rapid verification and disbursal:

  • Identity Proof: PAN Card, Aadhaar Card, Passport, or Voter ID.
  • Address Proof: Aadhaar Card, Utility Bills, or Bank Passbook.
  • Pension Proof: Pension Payment Order (PPO) original copy / details.
  • Bank Details: Bank statement or passbook copy showing pension credits for last 6 months.
  • Photographs: 2 passport-sized photographs of applicant (and co-applicant if applicable).

Lender & Approval Transparency Statement

Important Notice: LoanLoop is a loan facilitator and marketing channel partner, not an RBI-registered bank or NBFC lender. LoanLoop does not directly disburse funds or decide loan terms. Approval, interest rate, tenure, and disbursal are strictly subject to individual bank eligibility verification and credit policies.
