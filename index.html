About LoanLoop

Your Trusted Loan Channel Partner in AP & Telangana

Navigating banking procedures can be complex. LoanLoop bridges the gap between loan applicants and leading banks or NBFCs.

Who We Are & How LoanLoop Works

LoanLoop operates as a Direct Sales Associate (DSA) and referral partner connecting individual and business borrowers with partner banks and Non-Banking Financial Companies (NBFCs). We simplify the loan application journey by conducting initial profile reviews, assisting with documentation, and guiding applicants toward lenders matching their financial requirements.

Our service is 100% free for applicants. We do not charge processing fees or consultancy charges to borrowers; partner lenders compensate us directly upon successful disbursals.

Locations We Serve

We provide localized loan facilitation services across major urban, semi-urban, and rural areas in Andhra Pradesh and Telangana, including:

  • Telangana: Hyderabad, Warangal, Karimnagar, Nizamabad, Khammam.
  • Andhra Pradesh: Vijayawada, Visakhapatnam, Guntur, Nellore, Tirupati, Rajahmundry, Kakinada.

Eligibility Criteria Overview

While specific requirements depend on individual bank policies, primary eligibility criteria generally include:

  • Age: 21 years to 60 years (salaried) / 65 years (self-employed).
  • Salaried Individuals: Minimum net monthly salary starting at ₹15,000/month.
  • Self-Employed / Businesses: Minimum 2 years of operational history with audited financial statements or ITRs.
  • Credit Score: CIBIL score of 600+ considered; 750+ preferred for best interest rates.

Key Documents Required

Keep the following documentation ready to expedite your loan processing:

  • Identity Proof: Aadhaar Card, PAN Card, Passport, or Voter ID.
  • Address Proof: Utility bill, Aadhaar, or rental agreement.
  • Income Proof (Salaried): Latest 3 to 6 months bank statements & 3 months payslips.
  • Income Proof (Self-Employed): Latest 2 years ITR with computation, P&L statement, and 6 months business bank statements.

Lender & Approval Transparency Statement

Important Notice: LoanLoop is a loan facilitator and marketing partner, not an RBI-registered bank or NBFC lender. LoanLoop does not issue loans directly or make credit decisions. Final loan approval, applicable interest rates, processing fees, loan tenure, and credit limits are strictly at the sole discretion of the partner bank or NBFC based on credit assessment and verification.

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear details regarding loan eligibility, documentation, and processing.

Standard documentation generally includes PAN card, Aadhaar card, recent 3 to 6 months bank statements, latest 3 months salary slips for salaried applicants, or ITR with computation of income for self-employed individuals. Specific documentation requirements vary depending on the lender.
A CIBIL score of 750 or higher is preferred by most banks for approval and favorable interest rates. However, selected lenders and NBFCs consider applicants with scores between 600 and 749, subject to eligibility criteria and adjusted interest terms.
Personal loan eligibility is calculated based on monthly net income, age, current employer, monthly existing obligations (FOIR), total work experience, and credit score. Most lenders cap total EMIs at 50% to 60% of monthly income.
Turnaround times depend on the loan product and lender process. Personal loans are frequently processed within 24 to 48 hours following full document verification. Home loans and Loan Against Property typically take 3 to 7 working days due to legal and technical property appraisals.
Secured loans (such as Home Loans or Loan Against Property) require collateral like real estate or assets, offering lower interest rates and longer tenures. Unsecured loans (such as Personal Loans or Business Loans) do not require collateral and depend primarily on income stability and credit history.
