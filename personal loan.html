Frequently Asked Questions

Can I prepay my personal loan? expand_more

Yes, you can prepay your personal loan after a lock-in period of 6 months. Foreclosure charges may apply depending on the lending partner, usually ranging from 2% to 4% of the outstanding principal.

How much time does it take for disbursement? expand_more

Once your documents are verified and the loan agreement is e-signed, the funds are typically disbursed within 24 business hours.

Do I need to provide any security or collateral? expand_more

No, Personal Loans on LoanLoop are unsecured, meaning you do not need to provide any collateral, gold, or property as security.

What is the minimum CIBIL score required? expand_more

A CIBIL score of 700 and above is preferred for instant approval and lower interest rates. However, we also have partners who cater to applicants with lower scores.

Are there any hidden charges? expand_more

LoanLoop believes in complete transparency. All charges, including processing fees and EMI amounts, are clearly mentioned in the loan sanction letter before you sign.
